Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 28, 2026

At Arbahi DZ, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a functional affiliate marketing and rewards program, we collect information that helps us manage accounts and process withdrawals safely:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and a secure password.
  • Withdrawal Details: Necessary payment details (such as CCP, BaridiMob, or other local payout details) that you provide to request commissions.
  • Device & Log Data: Device type, operating system version, IP address, and application usage statistics to ensure security and prevent fraudulent transactions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to execute our affiliate system operations:

  • To register, verify, and manage your account.
  • To track referral links, calculate commissions, and credit your balance.
  • To process withdrawal requests and transfer payouts directly to you.
  • To identify, prevent, and block spam, double-accounts, and fraudulent activity.
  • To communicate account-related updates and handle support requests.

3. Data Security & Storage

Your security is our top priority. We implement robust measures to safeguard your information:

  • Encryption: All network requests between the app and the backend are encrypted using SSL/TLS protocols.
  • Hashed Credentials: Passwords and access credentials are hashed securely using industry-standard cryptographic algorithms.
  • Access Control: Data is stored in secure server environments with restricted access. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

We may collaborate with trustworthy third-party infrastructure and utility providers to enable email verification or cloud hosting. These services process your data securely and strictly in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

5. Your Rights and Deletion Requests

You have full ownership of your data. You have the right to request access to, edit, or permanently delete your account and associated personal details by contacting us directly at our support address.
